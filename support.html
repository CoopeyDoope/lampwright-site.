Support

Something not right? Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL and say which iPhone and iOS version you have. A screenshot helps.

Restore The Full Chart

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple ID that bought it. Family Sharing is on.

Get my progress back

Progress is kept in your iCloud account. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that Lampwright is switched on under Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Apps Using iCloud. It can take a minute to arrive after a fresh install. Achievements also come back from Game Center.

Game Center will not sign in

Sign in under Settings > Game Center (iOS 18: Settings > Apps > Game Center), then reopen the game.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
