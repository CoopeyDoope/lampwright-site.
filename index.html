Lampwright

Orb Roguelike for iPhone. Aim, bounce, light the sky.

Drag, aim, release. Your orb bounces, slows, and swells into a star. Your own stars are the obstacle and the fuel: nova them to chain a cascade across the board.

  • One thumb. Understood in ten seconds, a full run in about twenty-five minutes.
  • Your build is the sky. Every Constellation you earn is drawn into the night behind the board.
  • The board is an instrument. Bounces and novas play notes in each Sphere's key.
  • A new Daily Chart every day, free forever, with a Game Center leaderboard.

Free to download. No ads, no timers, no energy. One optional unlock, The Full Chart, opens Spheres II and III.
