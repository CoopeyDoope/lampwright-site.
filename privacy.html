Privacy policy

Lampwright, by Coopeydoope. Last updated 15 October 2026.

In short: Lampwright does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends anything to a server of mine.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, name, discoveries, achievements, best scores), your settings and any run in progress are saved on your iPhone.

iCloud

Your progress is also kept in iCloud's key-value storage, in your own iCloud account, so it comes back after a reinstall or on another iPhone. Apple stores it; I cannot see it. Turn it off in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Apps Using iCloud.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports your achievements and your Daily Chart score to Apple's Game Center. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's privacy policy, and your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Purchases

The Full Chart is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and I receive no payment details. The game only asks Apple whether you own it.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They do not identify you.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes what is on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Lampwright and choose Delete Data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
